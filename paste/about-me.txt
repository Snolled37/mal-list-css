[size=120][b]About my anime scores[/b][/size]
I use two scores, kept as tags on every title:
[list]
[*][b]Should Watch[/b]: would I recommend it to you? (shown as stars on my list)
[*][b]Review Score[/b]: how good it actually was, to 0.25. [i]?/10[/i] means I haven't rated it yet.
[/list]
My MAL score is a mix of the two.

[center][img width=798]https://snolled37.github.io/mal-list-css/dist/profile/score-guide.png[/img][/center]

[spoiler="What each MAL score means"]
[b]10[/b] – Like a 9, but as close to "perfect" as anime gets for me. A masterpiece.
[b]9[/b] – Great. Only tiny flaws, plus something extra an 8 doesn't have.
[b]8[/b] – Recommended. Solid basics without many mistakes. My Anime of the Year / of the Season picks are 8+.
[b]7[/b] – Like an 8 with more flaws, but the good still outweighs the bad.
[b]6[/b] – The good and the bad are about even.
[b]5[/b] – Like a 6, but boring.
[b]4[/b] – More bad than good. Watchable, but I don't recommend it; it's a waste of your time.
[b]3[/b] – Like a 4 with even less good. Please don't watch it.
[b]2[/b] – Almost all bad, with one or two interesting points.
[b]1[/b] – Watching it made me want to hang myself. Only watch it if you enjoy suffering.
[/spoiler]
